

# 🎶 Suno Songwriting

### Adaptive Framework for Lyric + Style Prompt Creation

*(for Suno v5 / Studio)*

---

## 🎯 PURPOSE

This document teaches how to write a **complete Suno song prompt** — combining structured **lyrics** and an expressive **style prompt** that adapts dynamically to the user’s artistic intent.
The goal is to make the AI behave like a **responsive collaborator**, shaping sound and lyric around your direction, not locking into formula.

---

## 🧭 CORE PRINCIPLES

1. **User Intent First:**
   Every Suno project starts from *the vision of the creator*.
   The AI adapts to your tone, genre, and emotion — not the other way around.

2. **Two-Part Structure:**

   * **Lyrics** = The story and emotional script
   * **Style Prompt** = The cinematic direction, describing how it *feels*

3. **Formatting Rules:**

   * Use **[ ]** for musical structure and instruments → `[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, `[Guitar Solo]`
   * Use **( )** *only* for background vocals or subtle ad-libs → `(oh)`, `(yeah)`, `(echo: “don’t go”)`
   * Do **not** use [ ] inside the style prompt — that section stays natural and narrative.

---

## 🧩 LYRIC STRUCTURE TEMPLATE

```
[Intro]  
Short instrumental phrase that sets tone or motif  

[Verse 1]  
Tell the story, use natural speech rhythm  
Mix long and short lines to create movement  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Raise tension — emotional or melodic lift  

[Chorus]  
Deliver the core message or hook  
Simple, repeatable, emotionally direct  

[Verse 2]  
Shift perspective or expand story  

[Bridge]  
Introduce contrast or revelation — change chord color, rhythm, or emotional lens  

[Break / Solo]  
Feature instrument (acoustic, electric, strings, etc.) or short instrumental re-center  

[Chorus – Final]  
Repeat and resolve — bigger delivery or softer echo depending on emotion  

[Outro]  
Closure — let last line or melody hang with intention  
```

---

## ✍️ CONTEMPORARY LYRIC TECHNIQUE

*(from Songwriter’s Compass & modern chart analysis)*

* **Show, don’t tell** — “I’m sad” → “The kitchen light hums and won’t turn off.”
* **Contrast sections** — conversational verses, soaring choruses.
* **Hook within 15 seconds** — the listener must feel the song’s core fast.
* **Emotional realism** — write like you’d text someone you miss.
* **Rhythmic variation** — balance short punchy lines with long flowing phrases.
* **Ground in truth** — each song should orbit one emotional thesis (“truth line”).

## 🎧 STYLE PROMPT TEMPLATE

Write this under 1000 characters.
No brackets. No artist names. Use descriptive, cinematic language.
Flow naturally through the song’s evolution — treat it like a sonic storyboard.

---

### Example Format

```
Style:  
Modern indie-folk ballad with fingerpicked acoustic guitars, brushed drums, and subtle synth pads. Begins with a quiet room tone and intimate mic feel, growing into a warm, rhythmic groove with layered harmonies. Male vocal is close, raw, slightly raspy, with micro-pauses at line ends and a gentle vibrato on sustained notes. Verses stay hushed and introspective, choruses rise with open chords and soft percussion lift. Bridge introduces a melodic twist and emotional breakthrough before resolving into a reflective, fading outro. Production feels organic and contemporary—clean mix, wide stereo field, and gentle analog warmth.
```

## 🧠 ADAPTIVE SONGWRITING LOGIC

When crafting your own Suno song:

* Ask: “What’s the emotional **truth line**?” — build every lyric around it.
* Decide: “What’s the **listener’s journey**?” — structure your dynamics to follow that arc.
* Adapt tone & rhythm to fit *the user’s world*: country → indie → pop → lo-fi → EDM → cinematic.
* Adjust **instrument focus** for user goals:

  * Acoustic-based → intimacy & storytelling
  * Electronic → energy & atmosphere
  * Hybrid → emotional build + drive

Your prompt should *always* reflect the user’s current creative context — genre, mood, energy, or message take priority over standard templates.

## 🧱 EXAMPLE: COMPLETE SUNO PROMPT

**Title:** *“Before It Disappears”*
**Theme:** Holding on to a fleeting moment
**Genre:** Contemporary folk / cinematic pop
**BPM:** 100

**Lyrics:**

```
[Intro]  
[Acoustic Guitar] breathes in quietly  

[Verse 1]  
You laughed like light spilling down the hall  
Said time’s a thief, then we lost it all  

[Pre-Chorus]  
The room still hums your favorite tune  
Feels like the air remembers you  

[Chorus]  
I’m chasing the sound before it disappears  
Holding my breath through the weight of years  
(ahhh) you echo through the fading reverb  

[Bridge]  
[Electric Guitar] bends slow, like memory folding in  

[Chorus – Final]  
I’m chasing the sound before it disappears  
[Strings] swell, but the silence wins  

[Outro]  
Fade out to breath and heartbeat
```

**Style:**
Reflective folk-pop with acoustic and electric guitars layered over warm pads and subtle percussive drive. Opens with fingerpicked intimacy, building tension through light rhythm and atmospheric reverb. Male vocal is emotive and unfiltered, slightly raspy in verses and open-throated in choruses with breathy background harmonies (ahhh). Song rises through organic instrumentation toward a cinematic swell, then dissolves into quiet heartbeat percussion and lingering air. Production feels modern and immersive, balancing raw performance with clean, dynamic mixing.

## ⚙️ ADVANCED STUDIO PRACTICE (v5)

* Use **[section tags]** for structure; describe *flow* in the style prompt.
* Build piece by piece: re-roll verses, keep the perfect chorus.
* Adjust sliders:

  * Weirdness 45–70 → creative risk
  * Style Influence 60–85 → focus adherence
  * Audio Influence 60–75 → if uploading stems/vocals
* For listener engagement: frontload emotion, clarity, and hook by bar 8.
* For authenticity: leave imperfections in phrasing or breath.
